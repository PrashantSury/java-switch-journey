> [!IMPORTANT]
> One mistake log per section. Add every **conceptual** mistake to the log of its section, and redo it after 3 days and again after 7 days. Cosmetic slips (typos, stale comments, missing braces, unused imports, stray `main()`) are fixed directly by Lumi and are not logged.

> [!NOTE]
> **Legend:** 🧩 DSA · 🌱 Spring · 🔁 redo · ✅ done cleanly · ❌ stuck again.
